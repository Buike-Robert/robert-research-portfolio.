02 / Featured research

Final-year project · 2026Successfully defended

Partially Autonomous Robotic Prototype for Ripe Fruit Detection and Harvesting

I designed and developed an intelligent robotic prototype to detect ripe fruit and position a robotic arm for automated harvesting, as my final-year engineering project at AE-FUNAI.

  • Developed a computer-vision pipeline using Python, OpenCV, and YOLO-based object detection to identify target ripe fruit.
  • Investigated stereo vision and triangulation for estimating the three-dimensional position of detected fruit.
  • Developed the processing pipeline from visual detection through spatial localization, coordinate transformation, and inverse kinematics to robotic-arm movement.
  • Integrated software and physical hardware, troubleshooting calibration, communication, and robotic motion.
